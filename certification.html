    Good to know

    What this certificate is, and is not

    The Foundation certificate is a self-paced, open-book knowledge check run in your browser. It shows you completed and passed the check. It is not a proctored exam, and credential IDs cannot be independently verified yet. Advanced certifications are planned to be assessed on applied work.
